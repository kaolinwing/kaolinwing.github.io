Frequently asked questions

Rules and moves

Q: Why am I forced to capture?

A: In English draughts capturing is compulsory. If any capture is available you must take one — quiet moves are simply not legal that turn. This is the rule that makes checkers a game of traps rather than a race.

Q: There are two captures available. Must I take the longer one?

A: No. English draughts lets you choose freely between available captures, even if one of them takes fewer pieces. (Some other traditions require the maximum capture; English does not.)

Q: My piece captured once and stopped, but I could have carried on.

A: If a man reaches the far row and is crowned, the move ends immediately — a newly crowned king may not continue capturing on the same turn. This is a genuine rule of the game, not a bug.

Q: Why can’t I move to a light square?

A: Play happens only on the 32 dark squares. Men move one step diagonally forward; kings move one step diagonally in any direction.

Q: How do I play a multi-jump?

A: Tap your piece, then tap each landing square in turn — the preview shows the path and rings every piece that would be taken. Tap the final square again to commit. You can switch to single-tap in Settings.

Score and time

Q: Why does my score keep going down?

A: Each game starts at 100,000 points. Win within one minute to keep them all; after that you lose 100 points per second. Playing well is only half of it — you also have to play fast.

Q: What are Gold, Silver and Bronze?

A: Tiers awarded on a win, based on your time: Gold within one minute, Silver within three, Bronze within five. The score meter marks all three thresholds.

Q: Does the computer’s thinking time count against me?

A: No. The clock runs only while the game is waiting for you, and stops entirely while the computer thinks. It also pauses when the App goes to the background.

Q: Beginner has no clock. Why?

A: Beginner games are untimed and always score the full time component, so you can learn the rules without the clock pressing on you. Every other level is timed.

Q: I won but my score is low. Why?

A: One of these: you took a long time; the difficulty weight is low (Beginner ×0.2, Intermediate ×0.6, Advanced ×1.0, Expert ×1.5, Pro A ×2.0); you played with a handicap (one piece ×0.8, two pieces ×0.6); or you used Retry — each retry multiplies your score by 0.75. A win also carries a material bonus between ×1.0 and ×1.5 depending on how far ahead you finished, counting kings as more than men.

Undo and retry

Q: Why do I have a different number of free credits?

A: It depends on difficulty: Beginner 3, Intermediate 2, Advanced 1, Expert and above 0. This allowance is shared between Undo during a game and Retry after a loss — spend all of it on undos and no free retry remains. Each undo adds 30 seconds to your clock.

Q: How far back does Retry go?

A: Four plies, which is two of your own moves, always landing on your turn. If the game is too short for that, it rewinds to the opening. The button shows which move you will return to.

Q: Can I undo in the Daily Challenge?

A: No. The Daily Challenge is one game per day with no undo and no retry — that is what makes the result mean something when everyone plays the same position.

Difficulty and unlocking

Q: Why are some difficulties locked?

A: Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced are open from the start. Expert needs 3 wins against Advanced, and Pro A needs 5 wins against Expert. Each locked level shows its condition.

Q: I won but my unlock progress didn’t move.

A: Games played with a handicap, and Daily Challenge games, don’t count towards unlock progress or your best score. They still count towards games played and win rate. This keeps unlocking a measure of skill.

Q: When will the “coming soon” difficulties be available?

A: Pro B and Master are planned for a future update.

Modes

Q: How does the Daily Challenge work?

A: One game per day. You choose between two opponents, and that choice settles the day — there is no second attempt. The date alone decides the opening and the computer’s play, so every player in the world faces the same game that day. No account and no network are involved.

Q: What are the puzzles?

A: Thirty endgame positions that you finish against the computer. Every one of them is a proven win, and a single wrong move loses it — the position is verified so that only correct play keeps the win alive.

Q: What is Rush?

A: Three minutes of back-to-back puzzles, generated on your device. Three mistakes end the run. Your best result is kept locally.

Q: How do achievements work?

A: There are 13. Each one is worked out from your game records and puzzle progress, so no achievement state is stored separately and nothing is uploaded. Clearing your records resets the achievements based on games played; the puzzle ones are unaffected.

Your data

Q: Will my history follow me to a new phone?

A: Yes, as long as both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and the App is allowed to use iCloud. Your game records and puzzle progress sync automatically; the game in progress does not. If they don’t appear, check Settings › [your name] › iCloud and make sure the App is enabled.

Q: What does “Clear all records” remove?

A: All game records and their transcripts, on this device and on your other devices via iCloud. Because your unlocked difficulties and most achievements are derived from those records, unlocked levels lock again. Puzzle progress and a saved game in progress are not affected. This cannot be undone.

Q: Can I delete a single game?

A: Not currently — only clearing everything is supported.

Q: Does the App need a network connection?

A: Not to play — everything works offline. A connection is only used to sync your records to your own iCloud. There is no account, no analytics and no data collection by us. See the privacy policy for details.

Languages and accessibility

Q: Which languages are supported?

A: Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, French and Brazilian Portuguese. You can switch language inside the App, independently of your system language.

Q: Can I turn off the animations?

A: Turn on Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion in iOS and the App disables its piece animations automatically.

常見問題

規則與走子

Q:為什麼我一定要吃?

A:英式跳棋規定「有吃必吃」。只要盤面上存在任何一種吃法,你就必須吃,該回合的普通走子全都不合法。這條規則正是跳棋以陷阱取勝的核心。

Q:有兩種吃法,一定要吃比較多的那種嗎?

A:不用。英式跳棋允許你在各種吃法之間自由選擇,即使某一種吃得比較少也可以。(部分其他規則要求必吃最多,英式規則沒有這條。)

Q:我的棋子吃了一子就停下,但明明還能繼續吃。

A:兵抵達對方底排而升王時,該手立即結束 —— 剛加冕的王不能在同一手內繼續吃。這是跳棋的正式規則,不是程式錯誤。

Q:為什麼不能走到淺色格?

A:對局只在 32 個深色格上進行。兵每次斜向前進一格,王可以斜向前後各走一格。

Q:連吃要怎麼操作?

A:先點自己的棋子,再依序點每一個落點 —— 畫面會預覽整條路徑,並用虛線圈標出會被吃掉的每一顆棋子;再點一次終點才真正落子。若你偏好單擊直接落子,可在「設定」中切換。

分數與計時

Q:為什麼我的分數一直在減少?

A:每局滿分十萬分。一分鐘內獲勝可拿滿分,超過之後每秒扣一百分。這是遊戲設計的一部分 —— 下得好還要下得快。

Q:金級、銀級、銅級是什麼?

A:獲勝時依用時給予的等級:一分鐘內金級、三分鐘內銀級、五分鐘內銅級。對局畫面的分數條上有三道刻度標示這些門檻。

Q:電腦思考的時間會算在我頭上嗎?

A:不會。計時器只在等待你落子時計算,電腦思考時完全暫停。App 切到背景時也會暫停。

Q:初學者為什麼沒有計時?

A:初學者不計時,時間分固定給滿,讓你先把規則學會,不必被時間追著跑。其他難度都會計時。

Q:為什麼我明明贏了卻只有很低的分數?

A:可能是下列原因之一:用時較長;難度加權較低(初學者 ×0.2、中手 ×0.6、高手 ×1.0、專家 ×1.5、職業 A ×2.0);使用了讓子(讓一子 ×0.8、讓兩子 ×0.6);或使用過「重來」,每次重來分數再乘 0.75。此外勝局會依終局的淨子優勢給 ×1.0 至 ×1.5 的加成,王的權重高於兵。

悔棋與重來

Q:免費額度為什麼不一樣?

A:依難度而定:初學者 3 次、中手 2 次、高手 1 次、專家以上 0 次。這個額度由「對局中悔棋」與「輸局後重來」共用 —— 悔棋用完之後,就沒有免費的重來可用了。每次悔棋會在計時上加算 30 秒。

Q:輸了之後的「重來」會退回多少手?

A:往回 4 個半手,也就是你自己的 2 手,並且一律停在輪到你的局面。若當時手數不足,會退到開局重新下。實際退到第幾手會顯示在按鈕上方。

Q:今日挑戰可以悔棋嗎?

A:不行。今日挑戰一天一局、不能悔棋也不能重來 —— 全世界下同一個局面,結果才有意義。

難度與解鎖

Q:為什麼有些難度是鎖住的?

A:初學者、中手、高手一開始就可以玩。專家需要擊敗高手 3 次,職業 A 需要擊敗專家 5 次。選單上會顯示每個等級的解鎖條件。

Q:我贏了,為什麼解鎖進度沒有增加?

A:使用讓子的對局與今日挑戰不計入解鎖進度與最高分,但仍會計入場次與勝率。這是為了讓解鎖代表實力。

Q:「敬請期待」的難度什麼時候開放?

A:職業 B 與大師會在後續版本推出。

模式

Q:今日挑戰是怎麼運作的?

A:一天一局。你在兩個對手之間擇一,選定即定勝負,沒有第二次機會。開局與電腦的下法完全由日期決定,因此全世界的玩家當天面對的是同一局棋。過程不需要帳號,也不需要網路。

Q:殘局解謎是什麼?

A:30 個殘局,由你接手下完。每一題都是已證明的必勝局面,而且錯一手就會輸 —— 題目經過驗證,只有正確的下法能保住勝勢。

Q:Rush 是什麼?

A:三分鐘內連續解題,題目在你的裝置上即時生成,錯三次結束。最佳成績保存在本機。

Q:成就是怎麼算的?

A:共 13 項,全部由你的對局紀錄與解謎進度即時推算,成就本身不另外儲存,也不會上傳。清除對局紀錄後,依對局計算的成就會歸零,解謎類的成就不受影響。

你的資料

Q:換手機之後戰績會跟著過去嗎?

A:會。只要兩台裝置登入同一個 iCloud 帳號,並允許本 App 使用 iCloud,對局紀錄與解謎進度會自動同步;進行中的對局不會。如果沒有出現,請檢查「設定 › [你的名字] › iCloud」中本 App 是否已開啟。

Q:「清除全部紀錄」會清掉什麼?

A:所有對局紀錄與棋譜,包含這台與透過 iCloud 同步的其他裝置。因為已解鎖的難度與多數成就是由紀錄推算的,已解鎖的難度會重新鎖上。解謎進度與進行中的續局存檔不受影響。此動作無法復原。

Q:我可以只刪掉某一局嗎?

A:目前只支援清除全部紀錄。

Q:本 App 需要網路嗎?

A:玩不需要,全部功能離線可用。網路只用來把你的紀錄同步到你自己的 iCloud。沒有帳號、沒有分析工具,我們也不蒐集任何資料。細節請見隱私權政策。

語言與無障礙

Q:支援哪些語言?

A:日本語、English、简体中文、繁體中文、한국어、Français、Português (Brasil)。可在 App 內切換,與系統語言無關。

Q:可以關閉動畫嗎?

A:開啟 iOS 的「設定 › 輔助使用 › 動態效果 › 減少動態效果」後,本 App 會自動關閉棋子動畫。
