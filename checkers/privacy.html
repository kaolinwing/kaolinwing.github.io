Last updated: 9 September 2026 · Applies to version 1.0

Checkers Pace (“the App”) is developed and maintained by Chien-Ting Liao (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. What we do not collect

The App requires no registration and no account. We do not obtain your Apple ID, name, email address or phone number.

The App contains no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software development kit of any kind. The only network traffic it generates is the synchronisation of your own game records to your own iCloud account, described in section 3.

We operate no servers and have no back-office system capable of reading your data.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device to provide gameplay features:

  • Game records: difficulty, the colour you played, result, tier, elapsed time, score, final piece and king counts, number of moves, undo and retry counts, handicap, the move transcript and how long you took over each move
  • Daily Challenge: the date of each day you played
  • Puzzle progress: which puzzles you solved, when, and how many attempts you needed
  • Saved game: a single game in progress, so you can resume it
  • Preferences: interface language, sound and tick toggles, two-step confirmation, whether to remember your last choice, and your best Rush result

All of it is stored on your device. Game records and puzzle progress are also mirrored to your own iCloud (section 3); the saved game in progress and your preferences are not. You can clear every record at any time from Me ▸ Statistics ▸ Clear all records — this also removes them from your other devices — or delete the App.

If you have iOS device backup switched on, your own backup may include this data. That backup is held in your Apple Account and managed by Apple; we cannot read it.

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have allowed the App to use it, your game records and puzzle progress sync to your own private iCloud database so that they survive a device change or reinstall. Sync is on by default.

This data lives in your own iCloud storage, protected by your Apple Account. We cannot read it, modify it, or delete it. It counts against your iCloud storage, not ours.

You can turn off iCloud access for the App at any time in iOS Settings; the App then keeps working with the records on that device only. There is no separate switch inside the App, so there is never a conflict between two settings.

4. Advertising and tracking

Version 1.0 contains no advertising SDK and shows no third-party advertising.

The App does not ask for tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency, because it does not track you and has no advertising identifier to share.

If a future version introduces advertising, we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, and say so inside the App.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not build user profiles, in this App or across apps
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, health data, microphone or camera
  • We do not embed any third-party library

6. Children

The App is rated 4+, contains no unsuitable material and collects no personal data. It is not enrolled in the App Store Kids Category. If you are a parent with questions about how the App handles data, please contact us at the address below.

7. Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, you retain full control of what is stored on your device:

  • Access: everything is visible inside the App
  • Deletion: clear all records from the Statistics screen, or delete the App
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud access for the App in iOS Settings
  • Portability: there is nothing held by us to transfer

If you are located in the EU, the UK, or another region granting data subject rights and still need assistance, please contact us.

8. Data retention

Data on your device and in your iCloud is retained until you delete it. We retain nothing, because we receive nothing.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “last updated” date. Material changes will also be announced inside the App.

10. Contact

support@kaolinwing.com

Technical note. The App’s privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares one required-reason API only: user defaults (CA92.1), used to store your preferences. Tracking is declared as false and no tracking domains are listed. Sync uses Apple’s CloudKit through SwiftData, writing only to your private database; the App links Apple’s Network framework solely to observe whether the device is online so that the Settings screen can show an honest sync status.

跳棋 Pace(以下稱「本 App」)由 Chien-Ting Liao(以下稱「我們」)開發與維護。本政策說明本 App 如何處理你的資料。

一、我們不蒐集的資料

本 App 不要求註冊、不需要帳號,也不會取得你的 Apple ID、姓名、電子郵件地址或電話號碼。

本 App 不含任何分析工具、追蹤機制或第三方開發套件。它唯一產生的網路流量,是把你自己的對局紀錄同步到你自己的 iCloud 帳號,見第三節。

我們沒有伺服器,也沒有任何可以讀取你資料的後台系統。

二、儲存在你裝置上的資料

本 App 會在你的裝置上儲存下列資料,用於提供遊戲功能:

  • 對局紀錄:難度、你執的顏色、勝負、分段等級、用時、得分、終局子數與王數、手數、悔棋與重來次數、讓子設定、棋譜,以及你每一手花費的時間
  • 今日挑戰:你挑戰過的日期
  • 解謎進度:已解開的題號、完成時間與嘗試次數
  • 續局存檔:一局進行中的棋局,供你之後接續
  • 偏好設定:介面語言、音效與提示音開關、二段確認、是否記住上次的選擇、Rush 最佳成績

這些資料都存放在你的裝置上。對局紀錄與解謎進度另會鏡射到你自己的 iCloud(第三節);進行中的續局存檔與偏好設定則不會。你可以隨時在「我 ▸ 戰績統計 ▸ 清除全部紀錄」清除,這也會一併移除其他裝置上的紀錄;或直接刪除本 App。

若你開啟了 iOS 的裝置備份,你自己的備份可能包含這些資料。該備份存放在你的 Apple 帳號中、由 Apple 管理,我們無法讀取。

三、iCloud 同步

若你已登入 iCloud 並允許本 App 使用,對局紀錄與解謎進度會同步到你個人的 iCloud 私有資料庫,讓你在更換裝置或重新安裝後能取回。同步預設開啟。

這份資料儲存在你自己的 iCloud 空間,受你的 Apple 帳號保護。我們無法讀取、無法修改、也無法刪除其中的內容;它佔用的是你的 iCloud 空間,不是我們的。

你可以隨時在 iOS 系統設定中關閉本 App 的 iCloud 存取權限,本 App 會改以該裝置上的紀錄繼續運作。App 內不另設開關,兩套開關就不會互相矛盾。

四、廣告與追蹤

1.0 版不含任何廣告 SDK,也不顯示第三方廣告。

本 App 不會透過「追蹤授權(App Tracking Transparency)」詢問你 —— 因為我們不追蹤你,也沒有廣告識別碼可以分享。

若未來版本加入廣告,我們會在該版本發行前更新本頁面與 App Store 的隱私標籤,並在 App 內告知。

五、我們不做的事

  • 不販售你的資料
  • 不建立使用者輪廓,無論是本 App 內或跨 App
  • 不存取你的通訊錄、相片、定位、健康資料、麥克風或相機
  • 不嵌入任何第三方程式庫

六、兒童

本 App 分級為 4+,不含不適宜內容,也不蒐集任何個人資料。本 App 未加入 App Store 的兒童類別。若你是家長並對本 App 的資料處理有疑問,歡迎透過下方信箱聯絡我們。

七、你的權利

由於我們不持有你的任何資料,你對裝置上的資料擁有完全控制權:

  • 查看:所有資料都在 App 內可見
  • 刪除:於「戰績統計」清除全部紀錄,或刪除本 App
  • 停止同步:於 iOS 系統設定關閉本 App 的 iCloud 存取
  • 可攜性:我們端沒有任何資料需要移轉

若你位於歐盟、英國或其他賦予資料主體權利的地區,且認為仍有需要協助之處,請透過下方信箱聯絡我們。

八、資料保留

裝置與你的 iCloud 中的資料會保留至你主動刪除為止。我們端不保留任何資料,因為我們沒有收到任何資料。

九、政策變更

本政策如有修改,我們會更新本頁面並修改最後更新日期。重大變更會在 App 內另行告知。
