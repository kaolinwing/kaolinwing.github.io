Reversi Pace (“the App”) is developed and maintained by Chien-Ting Liao (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. What we do not collect

The App requires no registration and no account. We do not obtain your Apple ID, name, email address or phone number.

We operate no servers and have no back-office system capable of reading your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device to provide gameplay features:

  • Game records: difficulty, result, score, elapsed time, move transcript, undo and retry counts
  • Endgame Puzzles progress and Puzzle Rush best score
  • Preferences: interface language, sound settings, tap-to-place mode
  • App version and device model identifier (used locally for performance and compatibility handling only)

This data is stored on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, also in your own iCloud (see §3). You can clear all game records at any time from the Statistics screen; this also removes them from your other devices, while Endgame Puzzles progress and an unfinished game are kept. Deleting the App removes what is stored on this device, but not your iCloud data (see §3).

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have allowed the App to use it, your game records, Endgame Puzzles progress and Puzzle Rush best score sync to your own private iCloud storage so that they survive a device change or reinstall. A randomly generated key, used only to detect tampering with your records, is kept in your iCloud Keychain for the same reason; it contains no personal information.

This data lives in your own iCloud storage, protected by your Apple Account. We cannot read it, modify it, or delete it.

Deleting the App does not remove this iCloud data. To delete it, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage, choose Reversi Pace and delete its data (wording may vary between iOS versions).

You can turn off iCloud access for the App at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Advertising

The App is free and displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob.

Google and its partners may collect the following to serve and measure ads, and for related analytics:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address (not GPS)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Advertising data and ad interactions (impressions, clicks)
  • App interaction data related to ads (such as app launches)
  • Diagnostic data (crash, performance and other diagnostics)

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. We cannot access it.
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

App Tracking Transparency. On iOS the App asks for tracking permission as required by Apple. If you decline, the App works exactly the same; you will simply see non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you allow tracking, Google may use the advertising identifier to show you personalised ads based on your activity in other companies’ apps and websites.

United Kingdom. If your device region or App Store country is the United Kingdom, the App always requests non-personalised ads, whatever your tracking choice.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not receive, store or use the advertising data described in §4; it goes directly to Google
  • We do not build user profiles (if you allow tracking, Google may — see §4)
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, precise location or health data

6. Children

The App is suitable for all ages, contains no unsuitable material, and does not itself collect personal data. It is not enrolled in the App Store Kids Category. If you are a parent with questions about how the App handles data, please contact us at the address below.

7. Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal data, you retain full control of what is stored on your device and in your iCloud:

  • Access: everything is visible inside the App
  • Deletion: clear all game records from the Statistics screen; delete the App to remove the data on this device; delete the App’s iCloud data in iOS Settings (see §3)
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud access in iOS Settings
  • Advertising data: please exercise your rights with Google, as described in their privacy policy

If you are located in the EU, the UK, or another region granting data subject rights and still need assistance, please contact us.

8. Data retention

Data on your device and in your iCloud is retained until you delete it. We retain nothing, because we operate no servers.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “last updated” date. Material changes will also be noted in the release notes of the next App update.

10. Contact

這份資料儲存在你自己的 iCloud 空間,受你的 Apple 帳號保護。我們無法讀取、無法修改、也無法刪除其中的內容。

刪除本 App 不會移除這些 iCloud 資料。若要刪除,請前往「設定 › [你的名字] › iCloud › 管理儲存空間」,選擇黑白棋 Pace 並刪除其資料(各 iOS 版本的字樣可能略有不同)。

你可以隨時在 iOS 系統設定中關閉本 App 的 iCloud 存取權限。

  • การลบ: ล้างบันทึกเกมทั้งหมดจากหน้าสถิติ ลบแอปเพื่อลบข้อมูลในอุปกรณ์เครื่องนี้ และลบข้อมูล iCloud ของแอปในการตั้งค่า iOS (ดูข้อ 3)
  • หยุดซิงค์: ปิดการเข้าถึง iCloud ในการตั้งค่า iOS
  • ข้อมูลโฆษณา: โปรดใช้สิทธิของคุณกับ Google ตามที่ระบุในนโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัวของ Google

หากคุณอยู่ในประเทศไทย สหภาพยุโรป สหราชอาณาจักร หรือภูมิภาคอื่นที่ให้สิทธิแก่เจ้าของข้อมูล และยังต้องการความช่วยเหลือ โปรดติดต่อเรา

8. การเก็บรักษาข้อมูล

ข้อมูลในอุปกรณ์และใน iCloud ของคุณจะถูกเก็บไว้จนกว่าคุณจะลบ เราไม่เก็บสิ่งใด เพราะเราไม่มีเซิร์ฟเวอร์

9. การเปลี่ยนแปลงนโยบายนี้

หากนโยบายนี้เปลี่ยนแปลง เราจะอัปเดตหน้านี้และแก้ไขวันที่ “อัปเดตล่าสุด” การเปลี่ยนแปลงที่สำคัญจะแจ้งไว้ในบันทึกประจำเวอร์ชันของการอัปเดตแอปครั้งถัดไปด้วย
