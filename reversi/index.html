Frequently asked questions

Gameplay

Q: Why is it not my turn sometimes?

A: When you have no legal move, the rules of Reversi require you to pass and your opponent plays again. The screen shows “has no move — passing”. This is not a bug.

Q: Why can’t I place a disc on an empty square?

A: A move must trap at least one unbroken line of opponent discs and flip them. Squares that trap nothing are not legal. The small dots show every legal move.

Q: What is “tap twice to place”?

A: By default the first tap shows a translucent preview disc and marks every disc that would flip with a dashed ring. Tapping the same square again commits the move. To place with a single tap, turn off “Tap twice to place” in Me ▸ Settings.

Q: What is Mistake analysis?

A: Tap a game in Me ▸ Statistics to open its replay. The App re-analyses the game on your device and, after a few seconds, shows Mistake analysis: an evaluation graph traces how the game swung, and red dots mark up to three of your costliest moves (only clear mistakes are listed; if there are none, it says “No clear mistakes this game”). Tap a mistake to jump to the position just before it; a translucent disc shows the better move. Step forward to see what you actually played.

Scoring and time

Q: Why does my score keep going down?

A: Each game starts at 100,000 points. Win within one minute to keep them all; after that you lose 100 points per second. Playing well is only half of it — you also have to play fast. Beginner is untimed: no time penalty and no tiers.

Q: Does the computer’s thinking time count against me?

A: No. The clock runs only while the game is waiting for you, and stops entirely while the computer thinks. It also pauses when the App goes to the background.

Q: What are Gold, Silver and Bronze?

A: Tiers awarded on a win, based on your time: Gold under one minute, Silver under three, Bronze under five. The score meter marks all three thresholds. There are no tiers at Beginner, which is untimed.

Q: What is rank?

A: Your scores from every game add up to your rank: you start at 10 kyu, climb to 1 kyu, then go from 1 dan to 5 dan — 15 steps in all. Daily Challenge, handicap and resumed games count too; only records that fail the integrity check don’t. The rank bar at the top of the App’s Home screen shows your current rank and how many points you need for the next step. Clearing all records resets your rank to 10 kyu.

Q: I won but my score is low. Why?

A: One of these: you took a long time, the difficulty weight is low (Beginner ×0.2, Intermediate ×0.6), you played with a handicap (1 corner ×0.8, 2 corners ×0.6, 3 corners ×0.4, 4 corners ×0.2), or you used Retry — each retry multiplies your score by 0.75.

Undo and retry

Q: Why is my free allowance different?

A: It depends on difficulty: Beginner 3, Intermediate 2, Advanced 1, Expert and above 0. This allowance is shared between Undo during a game and Retry after a loss — spend all of it on undos and no free retry remains. Each undo adds 30 seconds to your clock. Daily Challenge has no undos or retries.

Q: What happens when my free allowance runs out?

A: The allowance is per game. Once it’s used up you can’t undo or retry for the rest of that game: the Undo button shows “Undo 0” and is greyed out, and after a loss the result screen offers only “Play again” and “Home”. Your next game starts with a fresh allowance for its difficulty. (The Undo button is also greyed out while the computer is thinking, or when there is no move to undo yet.)

Q: How far back does Retry go?

A: Six plies (three of your moves) at Intermediate and below, ten plies (five of your moves) from Advanced up, always landing on your turn. If the game is too short for that, it rewinds to the opening. The line above the Retry button shows which move you’ll return to.

Difficulty and unlocking

Q: Why are some difficulties locked?

A: Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced are open from the start. Expert needs 3 wins against Advanced, Pro A needs 5 wins against Expert, Pro B needs 10 wins against Pro A, and Master needs 10 wins against Pro B. Each locked level shows its condition.

Q: Why doesn’t a resumed game count towards my best score, fastest win or unlocks?

A: A game is marked as resumed when you continue it from “Unfinished game” on the Home screen, or when the App spends more than three minutes in total in the background during it; the result screen then shows “Resumed game: doesn’t count toward best score, fastest win, or unlocks”. While you’re away you can study the board at leisure, so these games don’t count towards your best score, fastest win or unlocks, though they still count towards games played, win rate and your rank. Handicap games and Daily Challenge games don’t count towards best scores, fastest wins or unlocks either.

Data and sync

Q: Will I lose my history when I change phones?

A: As long as both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and the App is allowed to use iCloud, your game records, Endgame Puzzles progress, Puzzle Rush best score and Win Streak records sync automatically. Your preferences and an unfinished game are not synced. If nothing syncs, check the iCloud Sync status in Me ▸ Settings, and make sure the App is turned on under Settings › [your name] › iCloud in iOS.

Q: What does clearing all records (Me ▸ Statistics ▸ Clear) remove?

A: All game records and transcripts. Your rank, unlocked difficulties, game-based achievements and Daily Challenge calendar and streak are derived from those records, so they reset too and unlocked levels lock again. Endgame Puzzles progress, the Puzzle Rush best score, their achievements, the Win Streak records and an unfinished game are kept. Records on your other devices are deleted too if iCloud sync is on. This cannot be undone.

Q: Can I delete a single game?

A: Not currently — only clearing everything is supported.

Advertising

Q: Does the App have ads? What is the text at the bottom of the game screen?

A: No. Since version 1.1 the App has no ads. The Reversi tips and the rating invitation that rotate at the bottom of the game screen, and the occasional “Reversi tip” screen when you return Home after a game, are built into the App: nothing is loaded from the internet and there are no external links.

Accessibility

Q: Does the App support VoiceOver?

A: Every square on the board has a spoken label giving its coordinate and current state; for a legal move it also says how many discs would flip. Spoken labels on other screens are still being improved.

Q: Can I turn off the flipping animation?

A: Turn on Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion in iOS and the App automatically disables disc rotation and spring animations.

常見問題

玩法與規則

Q:為什麼有時候輪不到我下?

A:當你沒有任何合法落子位置時,依黑白棋規則必須跳過(Pass),由對手連續下。畫面上會顯示「無處可下,換手」。這不是程式錯誤。

Q:為什麼我不能下在空格上?

A:黑白棋的落子必須至少夾住一段連續的對手棋子並將其翻面。不能夾吃的位置就是非法步。畫面上的小圓點標示了所有可以下的位置。

Q:什麼是「落子二段確認」?

A:預設情況下,第一次點擊會顯示半透明的預覽棋子,並用虛線圈標出會被翻掉的每一顆棋子;再點同一格才真正落子。若想單擊直接落子,可在「我 ▸ 設定」關閉「落子二段確認」。

Q:什麼是失誤分析?

A:在「我 ▸ 戰績統計」點一局進入覆盤,App 會在你的裝置上重新分析這一局,幾秒後顯示「失誤分析」:優劣曲線畫出整局形勢的起伏,紅點標出你損失最大的手,最多三手(只列明顯的失誤;沒有的話會顯示「這局沒有明顯失誤」)。點清單裡的失誤會跳到那一手之前的局面,半透明棋子標出當時較好的下法;再往下一步,就能看到自己實際下在哪裡。

分數與計時

Q:為什麼我的分數一直在減少?

A:每局滿分十萬分。一分鐘內獲勝可拿滿分,超過之後每秒扣一百分。這是遊戲設計的一部分 —— 下得好還要下得快。初學者不計時,不扣時間分,也不評等級。

Q:電腦思考的時間會算在我頭上嗎?

A:不會。計時器只在等待你落子時計算,電腦思考時完全暫停。App 切到背景時也會暫停。

Q:金級、銀級、銅級是什麼?

A:獲勝時依用時給予的等級:一分鐘內金級、三分鐘內銀級、五分鐘內銅級。對局畫面的分數條上有三道刻度標示這些門檻。初學者不計時,所以沒有等級。

Q:段位是什麼?

A:每一局的分數都會累積起來換算成段位:從 10 級開始,升到 1 級後再從 1 段升到 5 段,共 15 階。今日挑戰、讓子與續玩的對局也會累積,只有未通過完整性驗證的紀錄不算。App 主頁上方的段位條會顯示目前段位,以及距離下一階還差多少分。清除全部紀錄時段位也會歸零。

Q:為什麼我明明贏了卻只有很低的分數?

A:可能是下列原因之一:用時較長,時間分被扣掉;難度加權較低(初學者 ×0.2、中手 ×0.6);讓子對局另有折扣(讓一角 ×0.8、讓兩角 ×0.6、讓三角 ×0.4、讓四角 ×0.2);使用過「重來」,每次重來分數再乘 0.75。

悔棋與重來

Q:免費額度為什麼不一樣?

A:依難度而定:初學者 3 次、中手 2 次、高手 1 次、專家以上 0 次。這個額度由「對局中悔棋」與「輸局後重來」共用 —— 額度全用在悔棋上,就沒有免費的重來可用了。每次悔棋會在計時上加算 30 秒。今日挑戰不能悔棋或重來。

Q:免費額度用完了怎麼辦?

A:額度以每局計算。用完之後,這一局就不能再悔棋或重來:悔棋按鈕會顯示「悔棋 0」並變灰,輸局後結算畫面只剩「再來一局」與「主頁」。下一局會依難度重新給額度。(電腦思考中,或還沒有可悔的一手時,悔棋按鈕也會是灰的。)

Q:輸了之後的「重來」會退回多少手?

A:中手(含)以下往回 6 手(你的 3 手),高手(含)以上往回 10 手(你的 5 手),一律停在輪到你的局面。若當時手數不足,會退到開局重新下。實際退到第幾手會顯示在「重來」按鈕上方。

難度與解鎖

Q:為什麼有些難度是鎖住的?

A:初學者、中手、高手一開始就可以玩。專家需要擊敗高手 3 次,職業 A 需要擊敗專家 5 次,職業 B 需要擊敗職業 A 10 次,大師需要擊敗職業 B 10 次。選單上會顯示每個等級的解鎖條件。

Q:續玩的對局為什麼不計入最高分、最快勝利與解鎖?

A:從主頁的「未完成的對局」繼續下,或對局中 App 在背景累計超過 3 分鐘,這一局就會標記為續玩,結算畫面會顯示「此局為續玩,不計入最高分、最快勝利與解鎖」。離開期間可以看著棋盤慢慢想,所以這類對局不計入最高分、最快勝利與解鎖,但仍會計入場次、勝率與段位。讓子局與今日挑戰局同樣不計入最高分、最快勝利與解鎖。

資料與同步

Q:換手機之後戰績會不見嗎?

A:只要你在兩台裝置上都登入同一個 iCloud 帳號,並允許本 App 使用 iCloud,對局紀錄、殘局解謎進度、限時連解最佳紀錄與連勝挑戰紀錄就會自動同步;偏好設定與未完成的對局不會同步。如果沒有同步,請在「我 ▸ 設定」查看「iCloud 同步」狀態,並確認 iOS「設定 › [你的名字] › iCloud」中本 App 已開啟。

Q:「清除全部紀錄」(我 ▸ 戰績統計 ▸ 清除)會清掉什麼?

A:所有對局紀錄與棋譜。段位、解鎖、對局類成就,以及今日挑戰的日曆與連續天數都是由紀錄推算的,所以也會跟著歸零,已解鎖的難度會重新鎖上。殘局解謎進度、限時連解最佳紀錄、這兩類的成就、連勝挑戰紀錄與未完成的對局則會保留。若開啟 iCloud 同步,其他裝置上的紀錄也會一併刪除。此動作無法復原。

Q:我可以只刪掉某一局嗎?

A:目前只支援清除全部紀錄。

廣告

Q:App 有廣告嗎?對局畫面底部的文字是什麼?

A:沒有,本 App 自 1.1 版起不含廣告。對局畫面底部輪播的黑白棋小知識與評分邀請,以及對局結束後按「主頁」返回時偶爾出現的「黑白棋小知識」畫面,都是內建於 App 的內容:不從網路載入,也沒有外部連結。

無障礙

Q:支援 VoiceOver 嗎?

A:棋盤上每一格都有語音標籤,會朗讀座標與目前狀態;可以下的位置還會讀出落子會翻掉幾顆棋子。其他畫面的朗讀仍在改進中。

Q:可以關閉翻子動畫嗎?

A:開啟 iOS 的「設定 › 輔助使用 › 動態效果 › 減少動態效果」後,本 App 會自動關閉棋子的旋轉與彈跳動畫。
