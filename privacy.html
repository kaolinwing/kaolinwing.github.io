Reversi Pace (“the App”) is developed and maintained by Chien-Ting Liao (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. What we do not collect

The App requires no registration and no account. We do not obtain your Apple ID, name, email address or phone number.

We operate no servers and have no back-office system capable of reading your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following on your device to provide gameplay features:

  • Game records: difficulty, result, score, elapsed time, move transcript, undo and retry counts
  • Preferences: interface language, sound settings, tap-to-place mode
  • App version and device model identifier (used locally for performance and compatibility handling only)

This data stays on your device. You can clear all records at any time from the Statistics screen, or delete the App to remove everything.

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have allowed the App to use it, your game records sync to your own private iCloud database so that they survive a device change or reinstall.

This data lives in your own iCloud storage, protected by your Apple Account. We cannot read it, modify it, or delete it.

You can turn off iCloud access for the App at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Advertising

The App is free and displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob.

Google and its partners may collect the following to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising identifier
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, approximate region)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. We cannot access it.
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

App Tracking Transparency. On iOS the App asks for tracking permission as required by Apple. If you decline, the App works exactly the same; you will simply see non-personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not use data for anything other than advertising
  • We do not build cross-app user profiles
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, location or health data

6. Children

The App is suitable for all ages, contains no unsuitable material, and does not itself collect personal data. It is not enrolled in the App Store Kids Category. If you are a parent with questions about how the App handles data, please contact us at the address below.

7. Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal data, you retain full control of what is stored on your device and in your iCloud:

  • Access: everything is visible inside the App
  • Deletion: clear all records from the Statistics screen, or delete the App
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud access in iOS Settings
  • Advertising data: please exercise your rights with Google, as described in their privacy policy

If you are located in the EU, the UK, or another region granting data subject rights and still need assistance, please contact us.

8. Data retention

Data on your device and in your iCloud is retained until you delete it. We retain nothing, because we operate no servers.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “last updated” date. Material changes will also be announced inside the App.

10. Contact

support@kaolinwing.com

黑白棋 Pace(以下稱「本 App」)由 Chien-Ting Liao(以下稱「我們」)開發與維護。本政策說明本 App 如何處理你的資料。

一、我們不蒐集的資料

本 App 不要求註冊、不需要帳號,也不會取得你的 Apple ID、姓名、電子郵件地址或電話號碼。

我們沒有伺服器,也沒有任何可以讀取你個人資料的後台系統。

二、儲存在你裝置上的資料

本 App 會在你的裝置上儲存下列資料,用於提供遊戲功能:

  • 對局紀錄:難度、勝負、得分、用時、棋譜、悔棋與重來次數
  • 偏好設定:介面語言、音效開關、落子方式
  • App 版本與裝置機型代碼(僅用於本機的效能與相容性判斷)

這些資料存放在你的裝置本機。你可以隨時在 App 的「戰績統計」畫面清除全部紀錄,或直接刪除本 App 以移除所有資料。

三、iCloud 同步

若你已登入 iCloud 並允許本 App 使用,對局紀錄會同步到你個人的 iCloud 私有資料庫,讓你在更換裝置或重新安裝後能取回紀錄。

這份資料儲存在你自己的 iCloud 空間,受你的 Apple 帳號保護。我們無法讀取、無法修改、也無法刪除其中的內容。

你可以隨時在 iOS 系統設定中關閉本 App 的 iCloud 存取權限。

四、廣告

本 App 免費提供,並顯示由 Google AdMob 提供的廣告。

Google 及其合作夥伴可能會蒐集下列資料用於投放與衡量廣告成效:

  • 廣告識別碼
  • 裝置資訊(機型、作業系統版本、語言、大致地區)
  • 廣告互動紀錄(曝光、點擊)

這些資料由 Google 依其自身的隱私權政策處理,我們無法存取。
Google 的隱私權政策:https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google 合作夥伴網站與 App 資料使用說明:https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

追蹤授權(App Tracking Transparency)。在 iOS 上,本 App 會依系統規定詢問你是否允許追蹤。若你選擇不允許,你仍然可以正常使用本 App,只是會看到非個人化廣告。你可以隨時在「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 追蹤」中變更此選擇。

五、我們不做的事

  • 不販售你的個人資料
  • 不將資料用於廣告以外的用途
  • 不建立跨 App 的使用者輪廓
  • 不蒐集你的通訊錄、相片、定位或健康資料

六、兒童

本 App 適合所有年齡層,不含不適宜內容,也不主動蒐集任何個人資料。本 App 未加入 App Store 的兒童類別。若你是家長並對本 App 的資料處理有疑問,歡迎透過下方信箱聯絡我們。

七、你的權利

由於我們不持有你的個人資料,你對本機與 iCloud 中的資料擁有完全控制權:

  • 查看:所有資料都在 App 內可見
  • 刪除:於「戰績統計」清除全部紀錄,或刪除本 App
  • 停止同步:於 iOS 系統設定關閉 iCloud 存取
  • 關於廣告資料:請向 Google 行使你的權利,方式見其隱私權政策

若你位於歐盟、英國或其他賦予資料主體權利的地區,且認為仍有需要協助之處,請透過下方信箱聯絡我們。

八、資料保留

本機與 iCloud 中的資料會保留至你主動刪除為止。我們端不保留任何資料,因為我們沒有伺服器。

九、政策變更

本政策如有修改,我們會更新本頁面並修改最後更新日期。重大變更會在 App 內另行告知。

十、聯絡我們

support@kaolinwing.com
